MOD 08

Module 8: Certification Prep & Final Practice

Theme: Ready for Assessment

Learning Objectives

  • Consolidate all Part 1 concepts into a single review pass
  • Complete one final, unscored practice crimp under exam-like conditions
  • Enter the final assessment with clear expectations and reduced anxiety

Arrival (8:30–9:00)

Open space for students to voice nerves about the upcoming assessment — normalize the feeling before reviewing material.

Review Session (9:00–10:30)

High-level recap across all modules:

  • OSI layers (recap from Module 1)
  • Cable standards overview (Module 2)
  • RJ45 pin order and crimping checklist (Modules 3–4)
  • Common mistakes recap (Module 6)

Final Practice Lab (10:45–12:30)

  • Students crimp their final "assessment-style" cable
  • Not graded — but run under the same process/conditions as the real exam
  • Instructor observes and gives final individual feedback
  • Test the cable and discuss results

Final Q&A + Expectations (1:15–2:15)

  • What to expect on Saturday 9 (exam structure)
  • Grading criteria walkthrough
  • Time limits per exam section
  • Open floor for questions
  • Confidence-building discussion

Wrap-Up (2:15–2:30)

Encouragement and dismissal — no new material introduced.

Check for Understanding

  1. Walk through the full crimping checklist from memory.
  2. What are the 3 components of the Saturday 9 exam?
  3. What's one common mistake you personally want to avoid tomorrow?

Home Assignment

  • Rest — seriously, no new crimping (risk of injury from over-practice)
  • Review notes only
  • Get good sleep before Saturday 9
  • Bring confidence and a good attitude
