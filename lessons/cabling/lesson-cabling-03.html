MOD 03

Module 3: RJ45 Connectors & Wiring Standards

Theme: The RJ45 Connector: Your Crimp Target

Learning Objectives

  • Identify the 8 pins of an RJ45 connector and their purpose
  • Recall the T568B wiring order from memory
  • Explain the difference between T568A and T568B
  • Explain when a straight-through vs crossover cable is needed

Quick Recall Quiz (8:30–9:00)

Open with a rapid pin-order recall check from last week's homework.

Lecture Notes (9:00–10:30)

8 pins, numbered 1–8 left to right when the clip faces down/away from you.

PinColor
1White/Orange
2Orange
3White/Green
4Blue
5White/Blue
6Green
7White/Brown
8Brown

Swaps the orange and green pairs relative to T568B (pins 1↔3, 2↔6).

  • Straight-through (T568B both ends) — connects unlike devices, e.g., PC to switch. This is what students will make ~90% of the time.
  • Crossover (T568A one end, T568B the other) — historically used to connect like devices directly (PC to PC, switch to switch). Modern switches auto-detect (auto-MDIX), but understanding crossover is still tested and useful for troubleshooting older equipment.

Wrong pin order can mean partial connectivity, wrong speed negotiation, or a completely dead cable — this is the #1 cause of "the cable doesn't work" in the field.

Lab 3A: RJ45 Connector Anatomy (10:45–11:30)

  • Examine RJ45 connectors with a magnifier
  • Identify the 8 pin contacts and where each wire seats
  • Discuss: why does order matter physically, not just electrically?

Lab 3B: Pin Order Practice (11:30–12:30)

  • Using a laminated diagram, arrange colored wires in T568B order
  • Practice with dummy (non-terminated) wire sets first
  • Repeat the sequence 5 times to build muscle memory — frame it as "color sequence is muscle memory," not just memorization

Lab 3C: Pre-Crimping Preparation (1:15–2:15)

  • Review cable construction diagram
  • Demonstrate proper cable hold and hand position
  • Show stripping measurement: 10–12mm of jacket removed
  • Safety briefing on crimping tools (no live crimping yet — that's next week)
  • Discuss failure modes in advance: uneven wire length, wires not fully seated, jacket stripped too far back

Check for Understanding

  1. Write the T568B pin order from memory (colors 1–8)
  2. When would you use a crossover cable instead of straight-through?
  3. What's the correct strip length before crimping?

Home Assignment

  • Write the T568B pin order from memory, 3 times
  • Watch a crimping tutorial video
  • Stretch and warm up fingers — crimping is physically demanding on day one
