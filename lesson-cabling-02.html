MOD 02

Module 2: Cabling Standards & Cable Types

Theme: Types of Cables in the Real World

Learning Objectives

  • Distinguish Cat5e, Cat6, and Cat6A cable specifications
  • Explain the difference between UTP and STP cable
  • Describe fiber optic cable at a basic level
  • Reference the EIA/TIA-568 structured cabling standard

Lecture Notes (9:00–10:30)

CategoryMax SpeedMax DistanceNotes
Cat5e1 Gbps100mStill common, budget option
Cat61–10 Gbps55–100mBest balance of cost/performance
Cat6A10 Gbps100mThicker, better shielding
  • UTP (Unshielded Twisted Pair) — standard for most office/home use, cheaper, twisting alone reduces interference
  • STP (Shielded Twisted Pair) — added foil/braid shielding, used near heavy electrical interference (factories, near power lines)
  • Uses light instead of electricity
  • Immune to electrical interference, much longer distances
  • More expensive, requires specialized tools to terminate
  • EIA/TIA-568 governs commercial building cabling in most of the world
  • "Structured cabling" = planned, labeled, standardized cable runs rather than ad-hoc wiring — critical for maintainability in real buildings

Lab 2A: Cable Identification & Inspection (10:45–11:30)

  • Examine different cable types (Cat5e, Cat6, Cat6A samples)
  • Feel the physical difference between UTP and STP
  • Measure jacket diameter, count twists per inch
  • Identify connector types on hand

Lab 2B: Cable Anatomy Video (11:30–12:30)

  • Cross-section view of a twisted pair cable
  • Discuss why twisting matters (cancels electromagnetic interference)
  • Discuss jacket materials and what they protect against

Afternoon Design Task (1:15–2:15)

Scenario: "A small office needs 50 network drops."

  • What cable category should they recommend, and why?
  • How many cables/meters are needed given the office layout?
  • Discuss trade-offs: budget Cat5e vs future-proof Cat6A

Check for Understanding

  1. What does "UTP" stand for and when would you choose STP instead?
  2. Which cable category would you recommend for a 10 Gbps office network?
  3. What standard governs commercial cabling installs?
