MOD 09

Module 9: Final Assessment & Certification

Theme: Show What You

Learning Objectives

  • Demonstrate crimping proficiency under timed, graded conditions
  • Demonstrate cable testing and verification skills
  • Demonstrate theory retention through a written assessment

Arrival (8:30–9:00)

Settle nerves, deliver final instructions and exam logistics.

Practical Exam Part A: Cable Crimping (9:00–9:30, 30 min)

  • Each student crimps 1 straight-through cable and 1 crossover cable
  • Scored on:
  • Proper technique
  • Professional appearance
  • Connector integrity (fully seated, clip intact)

Practical Exam Part B: Cable Testing (9:30–10:00, 30 min)

  • Students test their own cables
  • All 8 pins must register correctly on the tester
  • Results verified and recorded by instructor

Break (10:00–10:30)

Written Assessment (10:30–10:45, 15 min)

Quiz covers:

  • T568B pin order (write from memory)
  • Difference between straight-through and crossover cables
  • The 4 common reasons cables fail
  • Which OSI layer cabling operates at
  • Basic network device identification

Lunch + Results Scoring (10:45–1:15)

Instructors score exams while students wait/relax.

Results & Feedback (1:15–2:15)

  • Return graded exams with individual feedback
  • Award "Network Foundations Graduate" certificates
  • Group photo and celebration
  • Discuss next steps:
  • Continue directly to Part 2: Network Operations
  • Pursue CompTIA A+ Hardware certification independently
  • Explore entry-level cabling/tech jobs with this credential

Completion Pathways

After Part 1, students can:

  • Stop here and use cabling skills for entry-level tech roles
  • Pursue CompTIA A+ Hardware certification
  • Continue into Part 2: Network Operations: Configuration & Troubleshooting

Congratulations to all Network Foundations graduates.
