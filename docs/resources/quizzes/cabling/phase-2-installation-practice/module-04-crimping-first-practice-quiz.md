## Multiple Choice

**1.** What is the correct strip length for removing the outer jacket?

- A. 3–5 mm
- B. 10–13 mm
- C. 25 mm
- D. 50 mm

**2.** Which tool verifies continuity and pin order and is **mandatory** before installation?

- A. Cable stripper
- B. Wire cutter
- C. Cable tester
- D. Punch-down tool

**3.** When inserting wires into the RJ45 plug, the jacket must pass the:

- A. Gold contact pins
- B. Locking tab
- C. Strain-relief barb
- D. Transparent body

**4.** Why must the untwisted section of wire be kept short?

- A. To save copper
- B. To keep crosstalk low
- C. To make the cable flexible
- D. To fit the crimp tool

**5.** During crimping, the crimp tool should be squeezed:

- A. Multiple times, briefly
- B. With one firm, even squeeze until the ratchet releases
- C. As hard and fast as possible
- D. Only partway

**6.** What is the FIRST step in the 7-step crimping process?

- A. Trim the wires flush
- B. Insert into the connector
- C. Strip the outer jacket
- D. Test the cable

## Short Answer

**7.** Name two visual signs of a bad crimp.

**8.** What is the correct T568B order in which the wires are arranged after untwisting?

**9.** Explain why even pressure matters during the crimp.

---

## Answer Key

1. **B** — Strip 10–13 mm (module: 10–12 mm; field guide: ~13 mm) (§3, module §7-step process).
2. **C** — Cable tester is mandatory before install (field guide §2, module §Tools).
3. **C** — Jacket must pass the strain-relief barb so the plug doesn't pull loose (field guide §3 step 5).
4. **B** — Short untwisted run = low crosstalk (module §7-step process step 2).
5. **B** — One firm, even squeeze to ratchet release (module step 6).
6. **C** — Strip the outer jacket first (module §7-step process).
7. Uneven wire length, wires not fully seated, connector not fully seated, loose wires, or jacket trapped between conductors (module §7-step process step 7; field guide §5).
8. W-Orange, Orange, W-Green, Blue, W-Blue, Green, W-Brown, Brown (module step 3).
9. Uneven pressure means some pins don't get pierced properly → intermittent or failed contacts; even, firm pressure seats all 8 pins uniformly (module §Lab 4B, field guide §5).

> Difficulty: recall (Q1–6), apply/diagnose (Q7–9).
