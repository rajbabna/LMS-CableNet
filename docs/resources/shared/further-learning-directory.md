## 3. Practice & Simulators

- **Cisco Packet Tracer** — official Cisco teaching simulator (used in Part 2).
- **GNS3** — emulates real network devices for advanced practice.
- **Subnetting practice sites** — drills for the /25–/30 tables (repeat until
  instant).
- **wireshark.org** — the official packet analyzer with built-in sample captures.

---

## 4. Suggested Learning Path for Students

1. **Start** with the free CCNA course (study-ccna.com) — it matches our Part 2.
2. **Practice** subnetting until the /24–/30 table is instant.
3. **Build** small labs in Packet Tracer (2 switches, 2 routers, a server).
4. **Verify** with Wireshark / iperf3 on physical cabling labs from Part 1.
5. **Keep current** with TechTarget & BridgeCable blog for industry reality.
6. **Verify every fact** on Wikipedia — the free sanity-check reference.

---

## 5. Caveats

- Free tiers are sometimes **rate-limited** or cover **only basics** — expect to
  upgrade to labs/vendor training for deep CCNA practice.
- Sites update content; always confirm a URL before relying on it.
- Prefer official sources (Cisco, vendor docs) for certifications.
